  • New look – the dashboard has been redesigned to match the Rivers Trust's other data dashboards: a side panel for picking what to show, a full-height map, and the site summary opening on the right when you click a site.
  • Getting started tour – a short guided tour of each part of the dashboard. It's offered on your first visit, and you can take it again any time from the ? button in the top bar.
  • River schematics (new tab) – each river your organisation surveys drawn as an underground tube map style diagram, with its tributaries in the order they join and every survey site marked along the way.
  • Filter the map from the colour key – click a band in the key on the left (e.g. High or Very High) to show only those sites. Click it again, or Show all, to bring the rest back.
  • More league table views – as well as each site's average / median, the league table can now show each site's highest single reading, or the percentage of its readings that were High or Very High, colour coded from yellow (up to 25%) to dark red (over 75%).
  • Pollution summaries – the site panel now lists the pollution volunteers have reported at that site, such as sewage fungus, litter or oil, and how much.
  • New map layers – UKCEH Land Cover Map 2024 (hover to see the land cover type), and the water companies' Event Duration Monitoring returns for 2025, showing how often each storm overflow spilled.
  • Keyboard shortcuts – on the map, press R for the river network, F for flow direction, S for storm overflows and E for Event Duration Monitoring.
  • Zoom to your sites – choosing an organisation now zooms to its survey sites and switches on the river network and flow, instead of drawing the organisation's boundary.
